2. Mobile Application Output Screenshots (Proof of Implementation)

1. Image-Based UI & Screen-Load Entrance Animation

Features the illustrated BSIS 4C Campus Image Banner, custom icons, image-based action buttons (Play BGM, Tap SFX, Bounce, Fade-In, Slide), and automatic Fade-In entrance animation on screen load.

2. Audio Features — Background Music Playing & Button Tap SFX

Tapping 'Play BGM' toggles background ambient music ON ('🎵 Playing Ambient Loop') and tapping 'Tap SFX' plays the dual-tone 880Hz chime sound effect ('🔊 Image Button Tap Chime #2').

3. Embedded Short Video Player — Actively Playing

Tapping 'Play Video' starts the embedded Campus Orientation Video Player ('▶ PLAYING', time counter 00:09 / 00:30, live progress scrubber bar, and Pause/Restart controls).

4. Embedded Short Video Player — Paused with Controls

Tapping 'Pause Video' pauses playback ('⏸ PAUSED') and triggers the button tap sound effect ('🔊 Video Pause Chime (880Hz)').

5. Simple Animation — Interactive Bounce, Fade-In & Slide on Tap

Tapping the 'Bounce' image-based button triggers a spring bounce animation on the banner and studio card ('ANIMATION: BOUNCE EFFECT') while playing the tap sound effect.

6. Integrated Mobile Dashboard — Multimedia + SQLite Records

Scrolling down shows the embedded Video Player alongside the 4 Multi-Theme KPI Summary Cards and the SQLite Student Record Directory.
